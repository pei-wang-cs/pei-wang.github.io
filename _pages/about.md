I am a Ph.D. student in the [Formal Verification Lab](https://thufv.github.io/) at the School of Software, Tsinghua University. I joined the Ph.D. program in 2023.

My research focuses on formal methods for concurrent programs, with an emphasis on precise and efficient verification of concurrency bugs such as deadlocks and data races.

Research Interests
------

- Formal methods and software verification
- Concurrent program verification
- Deadlock and data-race analysis
- SMT-based and partial-order-based verification
