Hi! I am Pei Wang.

I am a Ph.D. student at the School of Software, Tsinghua University, and a member of the [Formal Verification Lab](https://thufv.github.io/). I joined the Ph.D. program in 2023. My research focuses on formal methods and concurrent program verification, especially precise and efficient analysis of concurrency bugs such as deadlocks and data races.
