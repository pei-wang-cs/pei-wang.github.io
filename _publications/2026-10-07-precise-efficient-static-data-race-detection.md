Summary
-------

**Deagle-DR** detects data races in concurrent programs by constraining the order of individual events, without instrumenting the program. The approach is precise within a bounded scope and comes with a correctness proof. Extensions to ordering-consistency solvers support race reasoning, while value-critical events help remove unnecessary constraints. Experiments compare the prototype with five existing race detectors and demonstrate competitive performance.
